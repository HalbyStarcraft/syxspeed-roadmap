Most mod overlaps are resolved automatically, down to part of a method

Shipped

When two mods change the same game code, the loader keeps both changes, piece by piece inside the method, instead of dropping a whole class. Songs of Blood and Empire + Syx Together: 12 of 12 shared pieces combine.

It only combines what it can prove is safe:
  • code built by different compilers counts as unchanged
  • added set-up lines, early exits, wrapped values, try/catch blocks, moved method bodies
  • "only in my mode" checks from two multiplayer mods; tiny pass-through helpers
  • a side panel shows what was combined; data files the game combines itself show in yellow
  • for each real clash pick "Use A" or "Use B", or turn one mod off, right in the panel
  • tested against all 745 Workshop items: 867 overlaps combine; 171 classes stay blocked across 30 mod pairs (21 mods); SyxSpeed clashes with nothing
  • next: SyxSpeed applies its tweaks on top of whichever mod owns the code (planned)

Save-file-based mod loading

The loader knows what each save needs and sets it up for you.

Load straight into a save with whatever mods it used

Shipped

Pick a save, and every mod it was made with is ticked. Untick one to load without it, then press "Launch with N mods and load save". Missing Workshop mods are downloaded first.

  • A save is always selected; the Saves tab is greyed out when you have no saves
  • "Continue" next to "Load save..." opens your newest save with its mods
  • The Launch button says how many mods will be on: "Launch with N mods" on Mods and Workshop
  • Same mods: the game just continues; different mods: one smooth restart
  • Respects the game: intro, music and the developer's news always show (the news line sits at the top of the launcher; only the restarted copy of a "load this save" start skips the intro it already played)
  • Closing the launcher with X exits cleanly

When a save won't load, see why and fix it in one click

Shipped

Loads in the game are plain vanilla. Only if a load fails because of mods do you see why, with the mods' names, and a "Fix in mod loader" button. It restarts the game into the loader with that save selected and glowing, where you can retry without the suspect mods.

  • Works even with the mod loader switched off; the button switches it on
  • Continue, Load, in-game load and quickload alike; the error itself is behind "Details"
  • "Copy error" / copy the conflict summary to send to a mod developer
  • Retry without one or more suspect mods: untick them on the Saves tab
  • Other errors still get the game's own error screen
